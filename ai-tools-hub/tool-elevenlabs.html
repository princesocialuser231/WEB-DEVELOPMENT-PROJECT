Open ElevenLabs

What it does

ElevenLabs converts text into natural-sounding speech in many voices.

Use cases

  • Voice-over for a presentation video
  • Audio version of your notes
  • Narration for a project demo

How to use it

  1. Sign up at elevenlabs.io
  2. Paste your text and choose a voice
  3. Generate and download the audio
