NotebookLM

Open NotebookLM

What it does

NotebookLM answers questions using the sources you upload, such as PDFs and notes.

Use cases

  • Summarise a textbook chapter
  • Ask questions about a research paper
  • Make a study guide

How to use it

  1. Open notebooklm.google.com with a Google account
  2. Add your PDFs, docs or links as sources
  3. Ask questions or generate a summary
