Suno

Open Suno

What it does

Suno turns lyrics or a short description into a full song with vocals.

Use cases

  • Make a song from your own lyrics
  • Create background music for a video
  • Turn a script into a short jingle

How to use it

  1. Sign in at suno.com
  2. Paste lyrics (ChatGPT or Claude can write them) and pick a music style
  3. Generate, listen and download your favourite
