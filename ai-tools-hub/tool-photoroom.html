Open Photoroom

What it does

Photoroom removes backgrounds and edits product-style photos in an app or browser.

Use cases

  • Clean the background of a product photo
  • Make a profile picture
  • Create a simple ad image

How to use it

  1. Open the Photoroom app or website
  2. Upload a photo and let it remove the background
  3. Choose a new background and export
