Gamma

Open Gamma

What it does

Gamma makes presentations, documents and simple web pages from a short description.

Use cases

  • Make a first draft of a class presentation
  • Turn notes into slides
  • Create a one-page project summary

How to use it

  1. Sign in at gamma.app
  2. Choose generate and type your topic
  3. Edit the slides, then share or export
