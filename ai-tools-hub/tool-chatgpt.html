ChatGPT

Open ChatGPT

What it does

ChatGPT is a chatbot from OpenAI. You type a request and it replies with text, ideas, explanations or code.

Use cases

  • Explain a topic simply or make practice questions
  • Draft an email or outline an essay
  • Turn a script into lyrics for a music tool

How to use it

  1. Open chatgpt.com and sign in
  2. Describe your task and who it is for
  3. Ask follow-ups and check facts yourself
